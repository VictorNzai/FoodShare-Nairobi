Content containers. .panel and .panel.panel--olive. Add your own padding and layout.

Form fields. Wrap a label and an .input (input or select) in .field. Put two fields in .field-row to sit side by side; they stack on phones.

One-time code. .input.input--code.

A choice between a few options. .segmented with buttons inside and .active on the chosen one.

Form submit, at rest and while a request is in flight. .pill--block for full width; setBusy(button, true) from /js/ui.js shows the spinner.

Inline message that stays next to the thing it is about. .alert.alert--success and .alert.alert--error. An empty alert takes no space.

Toast for the result of an action. Call showToast(message, "success" | "error" | "info") from /js/ui.js. Toasts appear at the bottom of the screen and dismiss themselves; errors stay longer.

Compact buttons for toolbars, table rows and dialogs. .btn with .btn--solid, .btn--quiet or .btn--danger. Group them in .btn-row. Keep the big pill for a page's main action.

Empty and error messages go in a .table-note cell.

A card with a table. .panel.card, a .card-head holding the .card-title and a .toolbar of filters (.input--sm), then .table inside .table-wrap. Use .num for IDs and dates and .cell-sub for a second line. Tables scroll sideways on phones.

ID Name Status Action

Loading. Content that is on its way is shown as skeleton bars in the shape of what will arrive, never as a spinner or "Loading..." text. From /js/ui.js: tableLoading(tbody, ["num", "text", "badge", "btn"]) for tables (placeholder rows the first time, dimmed rows on a refresh), skeletonCards() for a .card-grid, skeletonDetails() for a .detail-list and skeleton("60%") for a single line. An empty .stat-value shows a bar by itself, and .chart-box.is-loading covers a chart. Buttons keep setBusy().

Confirmation. await confirmDialog(message, { title, confirmLabel, danger }) from /js/ui.js resolves true or false. For other dialogs use <dialog class="dialog"> with .dialog-title and .dialog-actions.

Usage

Add the stylesheet lines to a page's head and the script before your own, then build with the tokens and classes above. 

<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com/" crossorigin />
<link
  href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Archivo:wdth,wght@62..125,100..900&family=Inter:wght@300;400;500;600;700;800;900&display=swap"
  rel="stylesheet"
/>
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/design-system.css" />

<!-- signed-in pages also load the app shell (sidebar + content panel) -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="/styles/app.css" />

<!-- before your page script: toasts, confirm dialogs, busy buttons, loading skeletons -->
<script src="/js/ui.js"></script>
